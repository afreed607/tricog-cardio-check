## Notes

- **Each industry page must solve a different problem.** If two pages differ only in the noun, retrieval systems treat them as duplicates and topical authority splits instead of accumulating.
- **Cite the setting-relevant metric.** High-risk sensitivity matters in an emergency department; low-risk sensitivity matters in mass screening, where false positives overwhelm follow-up. Choosing the right one demonstrates domain understanding.
- **"How this is handled today" is not a straw man.** State fairly what current practice achieves.
