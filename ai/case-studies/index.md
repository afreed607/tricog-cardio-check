## Healthcare teams using TCC

Amcare Hospital, Zirakpur · Healing Hospital, Chandigarh · Paras Hospital, Panchkula · EMC Hospital, Amritsar · RN Pandey Hospital, Uttar Pradesh · Asthma Bhavan, Jaipur · Suman Hospital, Sultanpur

These span multi-specialty private hospitals, regional hospitals and a specialist respiratory institution — settings whose common feature is high outpatient volume with limited on-site cardiology relative to that volume.

## Where deployments concentrate

TCC is most effective as a mass screening tool in outpatient department and medical camp settings, where rapid assessment of large patient volumes is the requirement. It is also used inside doctors' chambers as a point-of-care aid during consultation.

It delivers most in centres where clinical workflows are not clearly established, since a structured Low / Moderate / High output imposes a consistent process where none existed. Its portability also suits rural and geographically dispersed areas where running a conventional ECG is difficult: because TCC is a cloud service rather than hardware, the only physical requirement on site is the OMRON Complete device and a phone.
