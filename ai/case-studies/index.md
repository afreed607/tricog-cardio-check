## Framework

Every case study uses the same seven-part structure, so retrieval systems can extract comparable fields across cases. See the [template](../templates/case-study.md).

1. **Customer profile** — setting type, size, location, existing infrastructure, staffing
2. **Initial challenge** — the constraint before deployment, stated concretely
3. **Intervention** — what was deployed, how many devices, who operated them, over what period
4. **Results** — what happened, in numbers
5. **Metrics** — a table of measurable outcomes with definitions
6. **Lessons learned** — what transferred and what did not
7. **Related** — links to the industry page, product pages and any published article

## Commissioning briefs — DATA GAP

Cases that would most improve topic authority and citation likelihood, in priority order. Each addresses a question AI systems are currently asked and cannot answer from this site:

1. **A high-volume tertiary OPD.** The setting most of the workflow argument assumes, with no published case. Needed metrics: patients screened per day, escalation rate by tier, time-to-12-lead for High results, effect on queue throughput.
2. **A multi-site hospital network.** Answers "does this scale". Needed: consistency of escalation rate across sites, training time per operator, IT and integration effort.
3. **A corporate screening programme.** Answers "what does it find in an asymptomatic population" — the highest-interest unknown. Needed: cohort size, tier distribution, confirmed findings on follow-up.
4. **A rural PHC over several months.** Answers "does referral actually complete". Needed: referral completion rate, distance travelled, loss to follow-up.
5. **An insurance or pharma programme.** Answers scale and auditability questions.

For each, the essential and frequently missing element is **confirmed downstream outcome** — what the 12-lead and clinical evaluation found in patients TCC flagged. Screening volume alone does not evidence clinical value; confirmation does.
