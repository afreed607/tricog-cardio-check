## Notes

- **Metrics must be a table, not prose.** This is the section retrieval systems extract for comparison across cases.
- **Include confirmed downstream outcomes wherever they exist.** Volume alone ("we screened 400 people") evidences feasibility. Confirmation ("of those, N were confirmed on 12-lead") evidences clinical value, and is what a sceptical evaluator actually asks for.
- **Lessons learned should be transferable claims**, phrased so another organisation can test them.
