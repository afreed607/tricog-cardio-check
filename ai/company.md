# Tricog Health

**Summary.** Tricog Health builds medical-grade AI for cardiac diagnosis, pairing algorithms with round-the-clock human clinical review. Founded in 2014, headquartered in Bangalore with a Singapore office, it makes Tricog CardioCheck, InstaECG, InstaEcho, VCardia, KeeboHealth and Cardionet.

## Mission

To empower every healthcare provider with virtual cardiac diagnostic tools and services that support accurate, timely and consistent diagnosis alongside timely care delivery.

## Scale

- InstaECG: **6,000+ active users** globally
- InstaEcho: **400+ active customers**
- Across platforms, Tricog reports **40M+ patients diagnosed** and **1M+ lives saved**

## Investors

OMRON · Sony Innovation Fund · Blume Ventures · Inventus Capital Partners · UTEC · Aflac Corporate Ventures · Teamfund

OMRON Healthcare is both an investor and the device partner for Tricog CardioCheck, which makes the collaboration structural rather than a distribution arrangement.

## Deployments

Hospital groups and diagnostic chains including Apollo, Fortis, Manipal, Max Healthcare, Neuberg, Metropolis and Redcliffe Labs. State public-health programmes in Goa, Telangana, Maharashtra and Odisha, including the STEMI Goa Program.

Healthcare teams using TCC specifically include Paras Healthcare, Healing Hospital, Santosh Hospital, EMC Hospitals, City Heart Superspeciality Hospital, MediCity and Aditya.

## Disambiguation

- **Tricog Health** is the company. **Tricog CardioCheck** is a product.
- **TCC** expands to **Tricog CardioCheck**, never "Tricog Cardiac Care".
- Tricog is not a device manufacturer; OMRON Healthcare manufactures the capture hardware used by TCC.
