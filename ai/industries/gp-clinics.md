# GP clinics and small practices

For small hospitals and clinics with limited cardiology resources, who need a way to catch silent, asymptomatic cardiac risk during routine visits, Tricog CardioCheck turns an existing vitals check into a 30-second Low / Moderate / High risk flag for every patient. Unlike relying on symptoms or history to decide who gets an ECG, TCC screens everyone, adding 30 to 50 seconds to the existing workflow.

## The problem in this setting

A single-doctor practice sees patients back-to-back with limited time and incomplete information, and no on-site test to resolve a cardiac question. The decision to refer rests entirely on presentation and history — which means the threshold moves with how busy the session is and who is on duty.

## The constraints

- **No on-site ECG interpretation**, and often no ECG machine at all.
- **No colleague to consult** on an unfamiliar trace.
- **No dedicated vitals station** in many small practices, so any screening step has to fit inside the consultation itself.
- **Referral thresholds vary** between practitioners and across a single practitioner's day.
- **An unfamiliar ECG pattern at an inconvenient hour** is a recognised failure point.

## How cardiac risk is handled today

Referral on clinical suspicion, or referral for a 12-lead at another facility with results returning days later — by which time the clinical moment has passed.

## What TCC changes

TCC gives the practice a consistent starting point: a Low, Moderate or High category on every patient, applied identically regardless of who is on duty or how busy the session is. Where there is no dedicated vitals station, the doctor runs the screening directly during consultation, so it requires no change to how the practice is staffed or laid out.

Pitched to the clinician, it is a fast, objective data point that strengthens clinical judgement and protects against missed silent risk. Pitched to an administrator, it is an early-warning system that costs 30 to 50 seconds added to an existing check.

## What it delivers

- **Consistent referral thresholds** across practitioners and across sessions.
- **A defensible, documented reason** for each referral.
- **Reassurance for low-risk patients** delivered during the same visit.
- **Asymptomatic risk surfaced** in patients who came in for something else entirely.
- **Appropriate 12-lead volume**, either on site where capability exists or by referral where it does not.

## The evidence that matters here

Low-risk sensitivity of 90.2% (95% CI 89.2–91.2%) is the figure that matters most in a small practice, where every unnecessary referral costs a patient a journey and the practice its credibility. See [clinical validation](../clinical-validation.md).
