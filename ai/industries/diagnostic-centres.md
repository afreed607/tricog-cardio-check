# Diagnostic centres

**Summary.** A diagnostic centre handles volume and is measured on turnaround and consistency. TCC adds a cardiac screening layer at registration and identifies which walk-ins warrant a 12-lead before they leave.

## The problem

Patients arrive for a specific ordered test. Cardiac risk outside that order goes unexamined, even though the patient is physically present and a screening opportunity exists.

## Challenges

- Throughput pressure and fixed appointment slots
- Tests are performed to order, not to clinical judgement
- Interpretation capacity is the bottleneck, not acquisition
- Consistency across branches and shifts

## How this is handled today

Tests performed as ordered; cardiac assessment only where explicitly requested.

## The TCC approach

TCC screens at registration during vitals. Where a result is Moderate or High, the centre can offer a 12-lead in the same visit and route it through InstaECG for specialist-verified reporting within minutes, rather than asking the patient to return.

## Outcomes

- Cardiac screening added without a new appointment type
- Same-visit escalation from screening to diagnostic ECG
- Uniform standard across branches
