# Corporate and employer screening

**Summary.** Routine corporate check-ups reach a largely asymptomatic population — precisely the group conventional cardiac screening misses. TCC adds a cardiac risk category to an existing check-up without adding a specialist.

## The problem

Annual health check-ups measure blood pressure, weight and blood markers, but cardiac assessment is usually absent or limited to a questionnaire. Employees at real cardiac risk pass through without it being detected.

## Challenges

- Large cohorts, short time per person
- Asymptomatic by definition, so symptom-triggered testing finds nothing
- No clinical infrastructure at most screening venues
- Escalation must be proportionate, or the programme overwhelms follow-up capacity

## How this is handled today

Blood pressure and lipid measurement, sometimes a resting ECG for older employees, interpreted later if at all.

## The TCC approach

TCC attaches to the vitals station already in the check-up. Every participant is screened in the 30 seconds already spent on blood pressure. Only Moderate and High results escalate for a 12-lead ECG, keeping downstream volume proportionate.

## Outcomes

- Cardiac risk categorisation across a whole workforce in one drive
- Escalation limited to Moderate and High
- No specialist required at the venue
- Auditable, consistent standard across sites and years

## Evidence

High-risk specificity 92.2% (95% CI 91.6–92.9%) governs false-alarm burden, which determines whether a large-cohort programme is operationally viable. See [clinical validation](../clinical-validation.md).
