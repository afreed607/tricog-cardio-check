# Corporate and preventive health checks

For corporates and preventive health check providers running employee wellness screenings, who need to catch cardiac risk in an asymptomatic population that standard checkups miss entirely, Tricog CardioCheck adds a 30-second Low / Moderate / High risk flag to the existing health-check panel. Unlike standard wellness checkups that do not screen for cardiac risk at all, TCC adds it as a new line item with no added staff burden.

## The problem in this setting

Corporate health checks reach an asymptomatic working population — exactly the group conventional clinical assessment misses, because they never present as patients. Standard panels measure blood work, blood pressure and BMI, none of which assess cardiac electrical activity. An employee carrying an old infarction, ventricular hypertrophy or early ischaemic change passes the check without a flag.

## The constraints

- **Asymptomatic by definition**, so symptom-triggered testing finds nothing.
- **Large cohorts and short time per person.**
- **No clinical infrastructure** at most screening venues.
- **Escalation must be proportionate**, or the programme overwhelms the follow-up capacity it depends on.
- **Annual cadence** means a missed finding waits a full year for another chance.

## How cardiac risk is handled today

Blood pressure, lipid and glucose measurement, BMI, and sometimes a resting ECG for older employees — interpreted later, if at all. Cardiac risk assessment is usually absent from the panel entirely.

## What TCC changes

TCC integrates into corporate health programmes on whatever cadence they run — daily, monthly or annual — as a quick, non-invasive screening for every employee. It attaches to the vitals station already in the panel, so every participant is screened inside the time already spent on blood pressure.

Those flagged as Moderate or High risk are directed for further testing at a hospital, which keeps downstream volume proportionate and enables identification before a cardiac event rather than after one.

## What it delivers

- **Cardiac risk categorisation across an entire workforce** in a single drive.
- **Escalation limited to Moderate and High**, keeping referral volume manageable.
- **No specialist required at the venue.**
- **An auditable, consistent standard** across sites and across years, so results are comparable over time.
- **Early identification in a population** that would not otherwise be assessed until symptoms appeared.

## The evidence that matters here

High-risk specificity of 92.2% (95% CI 91.6–92.9%) governs false-alarm burden, which at cohort scale determines whether a programme's follow-up capacity holds. Low-risk sensitivity of 90.2% (89.2–91.2%) governs confidence in clearing the large majority who are well. See [clinical validation](../clinical-validation.md).
