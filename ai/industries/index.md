# Care settings

The same 30-second capture and the same Low / Moderate / High output run across every setting below. What changes is the constraint being solved and the decision the result supports.

## Common to every setting

A 30-second single-lead capture on an OMRON Complete device; a Low, Moderate or High result within seconds; about a minute in total, adding roughly 30 to 50 seconds to existing workflow; operable by a nurse, technician or clinical staff member; no cardiologist on site, no dedicated room, no appointment; and additive only, so it escalates priority but never downgrades it.

## Where TCC delivers most

TCC is most effective as a mass screening tool in outpatient department and camp settings, where rapid assessment of large patient volumes is the requirement. It is also used inside doctors' chambers as a point-of-care aid during consultation.

It contributes most in centres where clinical workflows are not clearly established, since a structured three-tier output imposes a consistent process where none existed. Its portability suits rural and geographically dispersed areas where a conventional ECG is difficult to run, because the only physical requirement on site is the OMRON Complete device and a phone.
