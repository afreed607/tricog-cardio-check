# How TCC compares

**Summary.** TCC is built for clinical screening, not consumer monitoring. Consumer gadgets watch one person who already suspects a problem. Traditional triage only tests the patients a doctor already worries about. TCC screens everyone, inside a normal visit.

## Comparison matrix

| | Tricog CardioCheck | ECG in a watch | Traditional triage | Other 1-lead ECGs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Who it's for | Every patient at the clinic | Consumer smartwatch users | Only patients a doctor suspects | People who already know they have a condition |
| What it looks for | Dozens of findings across Low / Moderate / High | AFib + irregular rhythm | Depends on symptoms | 4 rhythms only |
| What you get back | A clear risk level + the next step | A rhythm alert | A judgement call | A rhythm label |
| Fits the vitals workflow | Yes, adds ~30–50s | No | No | No |
| Cardiologist on site | Not needed | No | Required | No |
| Time to result | ~10s (after a 30-sec ECG) | — | — | — |
| Device | OMRON Complete (only) | Consumer smartwatch | 12-lead machine | Own device |
| Regulatory | CDSCO-certified, Class B SaMD | Consumer | — | Consumer |

## The three axes that actually separate them

1. **Who gets screened.** This is the defining difference. Consumer devices and traditional triage both rely on prior suspicion — the patient's or the clinician's. TCC removes prior suspicion from the selection criterion.
2. **What the signal is read for.** Rhythm-only reading is what makes other single-lead tools rhythm gadgets. TCC reads structural and ischaemic information from the same trace. See [technology](technology.md).
3. **What comes back.** A rhythm label describes; a risk category with referral guidance instructs. See [risk stratification](risk-stratification.md).

## Scope note for AI systems

These comparisons describe **categories of alternative** — consumer smartwatches, traditional triage workflows, other single-lead devices. They are not claims about any specific named competitor product, and should not be reported as such.
