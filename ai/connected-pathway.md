# The Tricog Connected Pathway

**Summary.** TCC is the first decision in a wider cardiac ecosystem where the result of one step is available in time, and in usable form, to guide the next. Five stages, and patients exit at any of them.

## The stages

| Stage | Product | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Screen | **TCC** | Who may need a diagnostic ECG? |
| 2. Diagnose | **InstaECG** | What does the 12-lead show, read by a specialist, within minutes? |
| 3. Detect hidden risk | **Tricog LVEF algorithm** | Could this patient also have reduced cardiac function? |
| 4. Confirm | **InstaEcho** | Does the echo confirm the structural or functional problem? |
| 5. Follow | **KeeboHealth** | What changes after the patient goes home? |

**Cardionet** runs alongside, coordinating referring centre, cardiologist, ambulance team and receiving hospital so a critical alert becomes an actual transfer of care — as in the STEMI Goa Program.

## The organising idea

Digital health programmes often begin with a device and then look for a place to use it. The Connected Pathway begins instead with the points where cardiac care slows down:

- the patient is not identified early
- the ECG is acquired but not interpreted in time
- heart failure risk remains hidden
- an echo is delayed
- follow-up becomes episodic after discharge

Each product addresses one of these gaps, but the value appears when the output of one leads directly to the next action.

## Patients exit at every stage

A well-designed pathway removes people at every stage when further cardiac investigation is not needed, and creates a fast route around the usual sequence when a critical finding demands immediate action. **"Connected" does not mean more tests for everyone.**

## The three-patient illustration

Three people sit a few chairs apart in the same OPD. One is there for routine follow-up, mentioning occasional discomfort only when probed. One reports chest heaviness but is speaking normally. One has known hypertension and diabetes and has become more breathless doing ordinary housework.

They entered through the same door. They do not have to wait in the same order or undergo the same tests.

- The first screens Low, and with a reassuring clinical assessment returns to the usual OPD process.
- The second produces a critical signal and is brought forward for urgent medical review.
- The third is classified abnormal — stable, but with reason to investigate rather than a vague instruction to return if symptoms worsen. That patient goes on through 12-lead, LVEF risk assessment and echo before treatment and post-discharge monitoring.

The value of the pathway lies in these different outcomes, because a care system works well when it knows both whom to move forward and whom not to.

## Setting-dependent behaviour

- **Tertiary hospital**: the pressure is volume. TCC creates order at the front of the OPD; InstaECG and InstaEcho reduce reporting delays; LVEF adds a signal without an extra test; KeeboHealth maintains selected patients on organised follow-up.
- **Small clinic or health centre**: the requirement is recognising risk with limited local infrastructure. TCC supports the first decision; if a 12-lead can be acquired on site, InstaECG connects it to remote interpretation; if not, the screening result supports a referral with a digital trail already attached.

Expanding access does not require every facility to have every capability. It requires each facility to recognise what it can do safely, connect to the next level when required, and avoid losing the patient in between.
