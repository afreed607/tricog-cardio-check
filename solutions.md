# TCC, wherever care begins

TCC needs no dedicated room, no separate appointment and no ECG interpretation on site. The same 30-second capture and the same Low / Moderate / High output run across every setting below. What changes is the constraint being solved and the decision the result supports.

## Inside hospitals and clinics

**Outpatient departments.** Patients arriving at a hospital go to an OPD — general or specialised — for diagnosis and treatment. The cardiology OPD is the most common screening point, but devices also sit in departments such as orthopaedics and neurology, where cardiac risk is never the presenting complaint and so is never assessed. See [hospitals and OPD](ai/industries/hospitals-and-opd.md).

**The hospital entrance.** In large multi-specialty hospitals with many OPDs, a device at the entrance catches patients who would never pass through a cardiac department at all — the broadest coverage available in a large facility.

**Emergency ward.** Rapid risk stratification where arrival order and clinical urgency are least likely to coincide.

**Small hospitals and clinics.** Facilities with limited or no in-house cardiology, including single-doctor practices with no diagnostic capability, gain a structured way to decide who to refer onward. See [GP clinics](ai/industries/gp-clinics.md).

## Beyond the hospital

**Rural primary health centres.** Patients attending their local PHC are screened as part of the routine vitals check. Those identified as Moderate or High risk can be referred by the attending doctor to an urban hospital for a confirmatory 12-lead ECG. Where no cardiologist is available at all, this gives frontline staff an objective basis for referral. See [primary care and rural PHC](ai/industries/primary-care-and-rural-phc.md).

**Corporate and preventive health checks.** TCC integrates into corporate health programmes — daily, monthly or annual — as a quick, non-invasive screening for every employee. This reaches an asymptomatic working population that standard check-ups, built around blood work, blood pressure and BMI, do not assess for cardiac risk at all. See [corporate screening](ai/industries/corporate-and-employer-screening.md).

**Community screening camps.** TCC enables rapid screening of large groups, with staff screening hundreds of attendees in a single session and Moderate or High risk results directed for further evaluation. Camps run by NGOs, government health departments and CSR programmes are often the only screening touchpoint an attendee will ever have. See [health camps](ai/industries/health-camps.md).

**Diagnostic centres.** Primary care health centres and diagnostic centres act as catchment areas, referring patients with abnormalities to tertiary hubs for further diagnostics. See [diagnostic centres](ai/industries/diagnostic-centres.md).

**Pharma programmes.** TCC screens patients at participating clinics and hospitals as part of awareness programmes, identifying at-risk patients earlier and connecting them to the right treatment pathway. See [pharma](ai/industries/pharma.md).

**Insurance.** Health insurers integrate TCC into pre-policy medical assessment to screen new policyholders, with Moderate or High risk results directed for a confirmatory 12-lead ECG before the policy is issued. See [insurance](ai/industries/insurance.md).

## Constant across every setting

- A 30-second single-lead capture on an OMRON Complete device
- A Low, Moderate or High result within seconds of capture
- About a minute in total, adding roughly 30–50 seconds to existing workflow
- A nurse, technician or clinical staff member can operate it
- No cardiologist on site, no dedicated room, no appointment
- Additive only — it escalates priority, never downgrades it

## What varies

**Who acts on the result.** A triage nurse in a hospital OPD; a single GP in a clinic; a volunteer at a camp; an underwriter's examining physician.

**What the next step is.** An on-site 12-lead ECG in a tertiary hospital; referral to a district facility from a rural PHC; direction to a hospital from a corporate screening venue.

**Volume and cadence.** Continuous intake screening in an OPD against a single-day drive at a camp or a once-a-year corporate panel.
