# TCC, wherever care begins

**Summary.** The same 30-second capture and the same Low / Moderate / High output run across eight care settings. What changes is the decision the result supports: sorting a queue in a high-volume OPD, or creating a defensible referral where no cardiologist exists.

One tool, every setting. Eight settings, one standard. No dedicated room, no appointment, and no on-site ECG interpretation required.

## The eight settings

| Setting | Constraint it solves | What TCC changes |
|---|---|---|
| [High-volume OPD](ai/industries/hospitals-and-opd.md) | Queue runs by arrival, not risk | Screens every patient at intake without slowing the queue, so priority follows cardiac risk |
| [Corporate screening](ai/industries/corporate-and-employer-screening.md) | Asymptomatic cohorts | Adds a cardiac risk category to routine check-ups, reaching the group conventional screening misses |
| [Rural PHC](ai/industries/primary-care-and-rural-phc.md) | No cardiologist on site | Frontline staff get an objective basis for referral |
| [GP clinic](ai/industries/gp-clinics.md) | No diagnostics on site | Single-doctor practices get a structured way to decide who to refer onward |
| [Health camps](ai/industries/health-camps.md) | No fixed infrastructure | Portable, usable by non-specialist staff, risk category available immediately |
| [Employer health programmes](ai/industries/corporate-and-employer-screening.md) | Whole-workforce volume | Screen a workforce in one drive; only Moderate and High go on for a 12-lead ECG |
| [Pharma initiatives](ai/industries/pharma.md) | Inconsistent screening quality | Scalable drives with a consistent, auditable standard |
| [Insurance](ai/industries/insurance.md) | Subjective risk assessment | Objective cardiac risk categorisation at onboarding and renewal |

## What is constant across settings

- 30-second single-lead capture on an OMRON Complete monitor
- ~10 seconds to a Low / Moderate / High category
- ~30–50 seconds added to the existing workflow
- No cardiologist required on site
- Additive only — escalates, never suppresses

## What varies

- **Who acts on the result.** A triage nurse in an OPD; a single GP in a clinic; a programme coordinator at a camp.
- **What "next step" means.** On-site 12-lead in a tertiary hospital; referral to a district facility from a PHC.
- **Volume and cadence.** Continuous intake screening vs. a one-day drive.
