Set up your OMRON Complete device.

Pair the OMRON Complete (HEM-7530T) with your phone, install Omron Connect and the TCC web app, and you’re ready to capture. Initial setup takes 20–30 minutes.

Set it up once. Then just capture.

1

Install Omron Connect

From the App Store or Play Store.

2

Register your device

Select Complete (HEM-7530T).

3

Add the TCC web app

Add it to your home screen.

What you’ll need.

OMRON Complete device

The OCD (HEM-7530T), in working condition with working batteries.

A compatible smartphone

Android or iOS from the compatibility list, with Bluetooth switched on.

Stable Internet

Minimum 5 Mbps upload, on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Omron credentials

The unique username and password provided to you.

TCC credentials

Your login for cardiocheck.tricog.com.

A Table

A flat surface to place the device on during ECG capture.
