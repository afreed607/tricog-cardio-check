About TCC

Tricog CardioCheck (TCC) is an AI-powered cardiovascular risk screening and triaging solution that analyses a 30-second single-lead ECG and categorises the result as Low, Moderate or High Risk.

TCC is designed to bring an additional layer of cardiac risk assessment to the point of care, helping healthcare teams identify people who may need closer clinical attention, including people who may not yet show obvious symptoms.

TCC is a CDSCO-certified Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) and is designed to work alongside clinical judgement, not replace it.
TCC turns a short single-lead ECG into an actionable risk signal.

It analyses the ECG waveform at multiple levels from fine waveform features and conduction patterns to broader rhythm patterns and uses these signals to classify cardiovascular risk into three
  • Low Risk - suitable for routine care based on the TCC assessment
  • Moderate Risk - requires timely clinical review
  • High Risk - requires prompt clinical attention

The purpose is not simply to generate another ECG report. TCC is designed to help healthcare teams prioritise who needs attention and how urgently.
Low Risk: The ECG does not show the risk patterns that TCC is designed to flag. The patient can generally continue through the routine care pathway, subject to clinical judgement.

Moderate Risk: The ECG contains patterns associated with potential underlying cardiovascular conditions and warrants timely clinical review.

High Risk: The ECG contains patterns associated with potentially significant cardiovascular conditions and should be prioritised for prompt clinical evaluation.

For example, High Risk with conditions such as atrial fibrillation and infarction, while Moderate Risk is associated with conditions such as ischemia and ventricular hypertrophy.
