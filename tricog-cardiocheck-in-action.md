# Tricog CardioCheck in action: where mission meets reality

For years, we've spoken about our mission: early detection and timely treatment of heart disease at scale. On June 8, that mission moved from idea to impact in one of the most meaningful settings possible the Village Clinic founded by my father, Dr. Ramana Rao.

This was our first large-scale field deployment of Tricog CardioCheck (TCC), our AI-powered single-lead ECG screening tool that classifies patients into Low, Moderate, or High cardiac risk.

### A legacy of care

Fifty years of Sundays.

Every Sunday for over five decades, my father has opened the doors of his Village Clinic on the outskirts of Bengaluru to hundreds of patients who may otherwise have had no access to care. His consistency, compassion, and clinical discipline have inspired generations.

For us at Tricog, this setting was not just familiar, it was deeply personal. It allowed us to bring Tricog CardioCheck into the real world and serve those who need it most.

### Our Objective

Screen at scale. Six OMRON Complete Devices screened patients waiting outside the clinic, while a dedicated station inside ensured no one was missed.

Two volunteers per point. One guided patient posture, collected details, and explained the result. The other logged the data and coordinated next steps.

Refer onward. Patients identified as Moderate or High risk were referred for a 12-lead ECG and further care.

Clinical decisions stayed with the treating doctors throughout. TCC's job was to sort the queue and surface who needed attention first, not to diagnose.

### What we witnessed

Several patients expressed relief after receiving a Low-risk result and encouraged family members to get screened. Others approached us with curiosity, eager to understand how the device worked and what it could tell them.

When my father and his team experienced the screening themselves, they shared thoughtful and encouraging feedback. Their appreciation of both the platform and the presence of our volunteers meant a great deal to all of us.

### By the numbers

Over 400 patients screened in a single day.

A complete care journey: TCC, then 12-lead ECG, then clinical consult.

Field validation of TCC's device performance, app sync, and workflow.

Rich feedback from patients, doctors, and stakeholders on usability and experience.

### Why this matters

This was not a test run. It was a moment of truth. A reminder that in healthcare, scale and science must stay rooted in trust and connection. That technology only becomes meaningful when it reaches real people, in real need, and in real time.

### Let's keep building

We will continue to refine Tricog CardioCheck and strengthen every aspect of the experience. But June 8 reminded us of what matters most: that purpose, when backed by people, creates lasting change. With science. With empathy. With intent.
