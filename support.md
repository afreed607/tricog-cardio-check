# Support

Support is reached at **support@tricog.com**, or through the contact form on the support page, which takes a name, email, optional phone number and message. Enquiries are routed by category.

## What each category covers

| Category | Covers |
|---|---|
| Screening not completing | The capture fails, or no result returns after a recording |
| App & setup | Login, credentials, or a result not appearing in the app |
| The device | The OMRON Complete monitor, electrodes, or signal quality |
| Order & shipping | Tracking, delivery timelines, address changes |
| Returns & refund | The returns process and refund status |
| Damaged or wrong item | An item that arrived broken or is not what was ordered |
| Billing & payment | Charges, invoices, GST, failed payments |
| Account & data | Access, patient data, privacy |
| Feedback / feature request | Product suggestions |
| Hospitals, networks & partnerships | Multi-site rollouts, bulk orders, media enquiries |
| Something else | Anything not listed |

## Before contacting support about a failed screening

Most capture problems come from signal quality rather than the device or the app. A clean single-lead recording needs the patient's fingers and thumbs held on the correct electrodes, and the patient sitting still for the full 30 seconds. Movement during capture is the most common reason a recording cannot be classified. The device also needs working batteries, and the phone needs connectivity for the recording to reach the Tricog cloud and the result to return.

## What a site needs to operate

An OMRON Complete device with integrated ECG, a mobile phone to view results in the Tricog CardioCheck app, and batteries for the device. No other capture device is supported. See [deployment](ai/deployment.md).

## Data and privacy

Tricog CardioCheck is designed with healthcare data privacy and security in mind and complies with applicable **HIPAA, GDPR and CDSCO** requirements. Access to patient information is restricted by authorised role, so sensitive health data is available only to those who need it.

ECG data captured on the OMRON device is transmitted securely to the Tricog cloud, where it is analysed and the risk result returned to the app.

TCC does not currently integrate with hospital information systems or electronic medical records. Results are viewed in the Tricog CardioCheck app.

## Institutional enquiries

Multi-site rollouts, bulk orders, partnerships and media go through the "Hospitals, networks & partnerships" category. Volume pricing, staff training, pan-India shipping and a priority support tier are available for multi-site deployments.
