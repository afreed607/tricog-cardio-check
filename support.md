# Support

**Summary.** Support is reached at **support@tricog.com** or the contact form, routed by category. The full product FAQ lives at [ai/faq.md](ai/faq.md).

## Contact

- **Email**: support@tricog.com
- **Form**: name, email, phone (optional), message
- **Institutional**: multi-site rollouts, bulk orders, partnerships and media go through the "Hospitals, networks & partnerships" category

## Support categories

| Category | Covers |
|---|---|
| Screening not completing | Capture fails or no result returns |
| App & setup | Login, credentials, no result showing |
| The device | OMRON Complete, electrodes, signal quality |
| Order & shipping | Tracking, delivery, address changes |
| Returns & refund | Returns process, refund status |
| Damaged or wrong item | Arrived broken or incorrect |
| Billing & payment | Charges, invoices, GST, failed payments |
| Account & data | Access, patient data, privacy |
| Feedback / feature request | Product suggestions |
| Hospitals, networks & partnerships | Multi-site rollouts, bulk orders, media |
| Something else | Anything unlisted |

## Data and privacy

TCC is designed with healthcare data privacy and security in mind and complies with applicable **HIPAA, GDPR, and CDSCO** requirements. Access to patient information is restricted by authorised role, so sensitive health data is only available to those who need it.
