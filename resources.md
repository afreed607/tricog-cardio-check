## What Is AI ECG Screening? Inside the Tricog CardioCheck Algorithm

What AI ECG screening is, why single-lead ECG is usually treated as a rhythm-only tool, and how TCC analyses a 30-second single-lead trace to return a Low, Moderate or High risk assessment. Covers what each tier flags, the reported performance figures (AUROC 0.909, 85.9% sensitivity for high-risk findings), and why the model supports triage rather than replacing the clinician.

## Where Mission Meets Reality: 400 Cardiac Screenings in a Single Day at a Village Clinic

An account of the first large-scale field deployment, at a village clinic outside Bengaluru founded by Dr. Ramana Rao and open every Sunday for more than five decades. Over 400 patients screened in a single day, moderate and high-risk patients routed straight to a 12-lead ECG and a doctor, and a complete care journey delivered in the field.

## From a Busy OPD to Care at Home: The Tricog Connected Pathway

The longest of the four. Follows three patients who enter through the same OPD door and leave on three different routes, then traces the full pathway — TCC, InstaECG, the LVEF algorithm, InstaEcho and KeeboHealth — explaining what each stage decides and why patients should exit wherever further investigation is unnecessary.

## What Is ECG Risk Stratification? How AI Can Help Sort ECGs by Risk

Why "abnormal" does not tell a care team what to do next. Sets out what ECG risk stratification is, how reported ECG interpretation accuracy varies by training level, what belongs in each of the Low, Moderate and High tiers, and how a care team uses the tier as the start of a workflow rather than a diagnosis.
