## Inside the Tricog CardioCheck algorithm

Why most single-lead algorithms only read rhythm, and how TCC reads the same 30-second trace at three temporal resolutions at once — fine-grained waveform morphology, medium-scale structural and ischaemic indicators, and coarse rhythm trend — fused before classification. Covers what each risk tier detects and why the model is additive only.

## Tricog CardioCheck in action: where mission meets reality

An account of the first large-scale field deployment, at a village clinic outside Bengaluru founded by Dr. Ramana Rao and open every Sunday for more than five decades. Six devices, two volunteers per screening point, 400+ patients screened in a single day, and a complete care journey delivered in the field.

## From a Busy OPD to Care at Home: The Tricog Connected Pathway

The longest of the four. Follows three patients who enter through the same OPD door and leave on three different routes, then traces the full pathway — TCC, InstaECG, the LVEF algorithm, InstaEcho and KeeboHealth — explaining what each stage decides and why patients should exit wherever further investigation is unnecessary.

## Beyond "normal" or "abnormal": every ECG as a clear call to action

Why a graded, actionable result beats a binary one. Sets out the three-tier risk framework, what belongs in each tier, and why risk stratification answers a different question from diagnosis: a diagnosis tells you what is happening in the heart, stratification tells you what to do next and how fast.
