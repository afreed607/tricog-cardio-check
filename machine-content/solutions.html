TCC, wherever care begins.

TCC needs no dedicated room, no separate appointment and no ECG interpretation on site. The same 30-second screening runs in a busy OPD, a rural health centre, a corporate check-up or a screening camp.

Solutions

Eight settings, one standard.

A nurse or technician runs TCC during the routine vitals check. The patient holds the OMRON electrodes for 30 seconds, the AI reads the recording in the cloud, and a Low, Moderate or High risk category returns on the app — the same way in every setting.

  • High-volume OPD:

    Screens every patient at intake without slowing the queue, so priority follows cardiac risk, not arrival order.

  • Corporate screening:

    Adds a cardiac risk category to routine check-ups — the asymptomatic group conventional screening misses.

  • Rural PHC:

    With no cardiologist on site, frontline staff get an objective basis for referral.

  • GP clinic:

    Single-doctor practices with no diagnostics get a structured way to decide who to refer onward.

  • Health camps:

    Portable and usable by non-specialist staff, with the risk category available immediately.

  • Employer health programmes:

    Screen a whole workforce in one drive; only Moderate and High risk go on for a 12-lead ECG.

  • Pharma initiatives:

    Run screening drives at scale with one consistent, auditable risk standard across every site.

  • Insurance:

    An objective cardiac risk category at onboarding or renewal, captured in under a minute.
