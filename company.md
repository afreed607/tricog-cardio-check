# Tricog Health

Tricog Health builds medical-grade AI for cardiac diagnosis, pairing algorithms with round-the-clock human clinical review. Founded in 2014, it is headquartered in Bangalore with an office in Singapore, and makes Tricog CardioCheck, InstaECG, InstaEcho, the Tricog LVEF algorithm, KeeboHealth, VCardia and Cardionet.

## Mission

To empower every healthcare provider with virtual cardiac diagnostic tools and services that support accurate, timely and consistent diagnosis alongside timely care delivery.

## Origin of Tricog CardioCheck

TCC grew out of an observation by Tricog's leadership that too many cardiac patients are identified too late. Drawn from personal experience and a close reading of the cardiac care landscape in India, the founding insight was that if cardiovascular risk could be detected earlier — at the point of routine care, before symptoms escalate — lives could be saved.

The product vision that followed is to make cardiac risk screening a routine part of every patient's clinical journey: catching patients who would otherwise be missed, guiding them toward timely care, and giving healthcare professionals a faster way to prioritise who needs attention most.

## The data asset

Tricog's twelve years of clinical operation have produced a dataset of 12-lead ECGs that is among the largest and most diverse cardiac datasets in India. That dataset is the foundation the TCC algorithm was trained on, and its breadth was instrumental in reaching accuracy that meets clinical and regulatory standards. It is the principal reason a company of Tricog's size can build a screening model of this scope.

## Scale

InstaECG has more than 6,000 active users globally; InstaEcho more than 400 active customers. Across its platforms Tricog reports more than 40 million patients diagnosed and more than 1 million lives saved.

## Investors

OMRON, Sony Innovation Fund, Blume Ventures, Inventus Capital Partners, UTEC, Aflac Corporate Ventures and Teamfund.

OMRON Healthcare is both an investor in Tricog and the device partner for Tricog CardioCheck, supplying the ECG-enabled Complete blood pressure monitor on which TCC screenings are captured.

## Deployments

Hospital groups and diagnostic chains including Apollo, Fortis, Manipal, Max Healthcare, Neuberg, Metropolis and Redcliffe Labs. State public-health programmes in Goa, Telangana, Maharashtra and Odisha, including the STEMI Goa Program, which coordinates referring centres, cardiologists, ambulance teams and receiving hospitals so a critical alert results in an actual transfer of care.

Healthcare teams using Tricog CardioCheck include Amcare Hospital in Zirakpur, Healing Hospital in Chandigarh, Paras Hospital in Panchkula, EMC Hospital in Amritsar, RN Pandey Hospital in Uttar Pradesh, Asthma Bhavan in Jaipur and Suman Hospital in Sultanpur.

## Naming

Tricog Health is the company; Tricog CardioCheck is a product. TCC abbreviates Tricog CardioCheck. Tricog does not manufacture hardware — OMRON Healthcare manufactures the capture device used by TCC.
